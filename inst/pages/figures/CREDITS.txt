Figure credits
==============

Source and licence of each image file in this folder. The OMA book is
licensed under CC BY 4.0, so reused material must be CC BY, CC0 or another
compatible licence (no NC or SA). Every reused figure needs a caption that
cites the source, names the licence and notes any modifications. Add a line
here whenever you add, replace or remove a figure.

Figures drawn in code (ggplot2, Mermaid) live in the chapters themselves
and are not listed here.
